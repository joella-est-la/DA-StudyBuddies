Academic Standards and Program Overview

Dakar Academy StudyBuddies connects DA families with upperclassman tutors ready to lend a hand. Whether it's tackling tricky homework or building better study habits, our goal is to match your child with a helpful DA student who can guide them through it.

Student Mentors

Upperclassmen tutors share their knowledge across various subject areas and assist younger students during dedicated after-school slots.

Tutor Verification

Student tutors must register using an official student email address ending in @dakar-academy.org.

Integrity and Accountability

Tutors are expected to be punctual, respectful, and committed to helping younger learners succeed in a supportive environment.

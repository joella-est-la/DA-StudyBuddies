Academic Standards and Honor Code

Welcome to PeerConnect. To maintain high-quality instruction and safety within the Dakar Academy community, all peer tutors must review and fulfill the following requirements before registering:

Grade Requirements

Tutors must have achieved an A or B (or maintain a 3.5 GPA minimum) in any subject area they wish to tutor.

Domain Restrictions

Only verified student email accounts ending in @dakar-academy.org are permitted to join the platform.

Integrity and Accountability

Tutors must be punctual, respectful, and dedicated to helping their peers succeed while upholding school behavioral expectations.
